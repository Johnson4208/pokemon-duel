    NEW TRAINER?

    Your first battle in 3 steps.

    1. Choose 3 Pokémon.

      Your first Pokémon enters the arena first. The faster Pokémon takes the first turn.

    2. Create a room and share the code or link.

      Your friend opens this page and joins your room. Both players select “Ready to battle” to begin. The host must keep the page open.

    3. Take turns to battle.

      Each Pokémon has an Ultimate marked ✦: 55% stronger damage (or 40% HP healing for support Pokémon), followed by a cooldown of 3 of your turns. Choose a move, switch Pokémon, guard, or use your one potion. A fainted Pokémon is replaced automatically. Defeat all 3 opponents to win.

    These are simplified rules: each Pokémon has one type, and stats are balanced for this game.

    Type advantages used in this game

    Normal moves do not affect Ghost types; Ghost moves do not affect Normal types; Electric moves do not affect Ground types. Move buttons show the current effectiveness multiplier.

    Having trouble connecting?

    Try a new code, check that the host still has the page open, or try a different network. NAT or firewall settings may block direct connections; the project owner may need to configure TURN. Practice against the computer is still available.
