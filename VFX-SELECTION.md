# Effect selection and direction

All eight supplied archives were extracted and compared as sequences, with transparent bounds normalized and nearest-neighbour scaling. Selection is based on readable silhouettes, strong impact frames, motion variety, and suitability beside pixel Pokémon.

| Pack | Comparison | Use in the game |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mega Pack VFX RPG Lite | Broadest selection of area effects, portals, elemental terrain and support. Some sequences are subdued without a stronger impact layer. | Environmental staging, runes, rising spires, vortices and legendary accents. |
| Pixel Combat VFX Free Sample | Strongest detailed lightning, water lash, fireball and ground impact among these samples. | Main strike and impact layers; not reused as one universal explosion. |
| Free Pixel Effects Pack | Strong, colourful explosions and cosmic effects. Some have long empty tails. | Fire Blast, psychic storms, cosmic impacts, phantom stars; retimed to the impact phase. |
| Warped Shooting FX | Clear, energetic projectile silhouettes and beam shapes. Blue palette needs adaptation for electric skills. | Beams, pulse shots, energy balls and golden Pikachu variants. |
| Pixel VFX Essentials | Compact, readable slash and impact shapes; useful supplementary elemental effects. | Multi-hit attacks, claws, flame streams, frost sparks and ambient residue. |
| Effects Pack 12 | Colourful orbiting rings; small original 32-pixel detail. | Charging rings, cosmic rotation and time distortion. |
| Impact Effects | Thin bursts work well at contact but lack the scale for a legendary main effect. | Secondary contact sparks, especially claw strikes. |
| Pixel VFX Vol1 Lite | Clean simple silhouettes; good support graphics rather than dominant ultimate attacks. | Shields, healing particles and minor impacts. |

## Composition

97 selected sequences in seven runtime atlases. Each action has sixteen stepped frames with distinct charge, delivery, impact and residue phases. Original archives and standalone samples are not shipped.

Twelve offensive delivery patterns: projectile, beam, sky strike, meteor rain, target eruption, vortex, sweeping wave, direct target magic, multi-direction barrage, dash, slash and travelling ground shock. Three support patterns: heal, guard and focus.

Thunder, Thunderbolt and Quick Attack use different staging and golden electric accents. Legendary signature attacks use named recipes and species-specific environmental accents. Impact reactions differ between lift, slam, push and repeated cuts. Misses omit target explosions. Battle metadata preserves the original attacking species after a faint or switch.

The preview uses the same renderer and displays each ability's delivery pattern. Reduced-motion mode shows one static impact frame. Practice AI waits for the full 1.6-second effect before taking its turn.
